WUC Europe GmbH is based in Frankfurt am Main. Backed by our team’s 20 years of industry expertise, we link China and Europe via road, sea, air and rail freight, offering full customs services and multilingual support. We specialise in automotive logistics and exhibition services.

What we move, and how

Three lanes

Road

LKW cargo runs across Germany and into Poland, France, the Czech Republic, and beyond — our core lane for messe deliveries and cross-border freight.

Sea

We deliver efficient ocean freight with door delivery at EU destination ports.

Our services:

- FCL / LCL pre-carriage & onward carriage

- Customs clearance & port/document handling (standard clearance, T1 transit, fiscal customs clearance)

- Intermodal & direct trucking

- Primary coverage: Hamburg and Rotterdam

Warehousing

We offer storage and cross-docking at our Nuremberg and Hamburg hubs, with forklift capacity for cargo up to 5 tons.

Where we operate

Eight countries, one coordinated route.

From our Augsburg and Hamburg hubs, we run regular lanes into China, Poland, France, the Czech Republic, Hungary, Belgium, the Netherlands, and Italy — with German-Messe support built into every route.
